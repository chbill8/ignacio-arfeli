About

I bring eight years of experience as a producer, mixer and mastering engineer, specialising in techno and its subgenres.

More than fifty of my tracks have entered the Beatport top 100, officially tracked by Songstats. I've released on Drumcode, Filth on Acid and Terminal M, and my records are played by the DJs who define the sound: Adam Beyer, Amelie Lens, Charlotte de Witte, Enrico Sangiuliano and Eric Prydz among them.

The same ears and the same room are available for your music. Whether you need a track built from scratch, a mix that hits, or a master that holds up on a big system, you'll get honest feedback and a record that's ready to send.
